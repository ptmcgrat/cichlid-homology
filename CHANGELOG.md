# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- Repository scaffold (package `cichlid_homology`, console script `cichlid-homology`).
- `read.py`: one in-memory form (`Family`) for a family folder of any method; leaf parsing,
  alignment/leaf consistency checks, rooting as recorded in `done.json`.
- `cichlid-homology survey`: read-only survey of a sample of families for one focal species.
- Offline tests on constructed family folders.
- No rooting, orthology calls or output tables yet (pilot not started).
